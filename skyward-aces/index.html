Skyward Aces

Pilot, the islands around Port Hollis are under attack. Fly the campaign to push the enemy back, or take on endless waves to chase a high score.

Turn your phone sideways to play.
Touch: drag the left side to steer. With invert pitch on, pull down to climb and push up to dive. Guns fire by themselves when an enemy is in the sight; turn off auto-fire guns (here or with the AUTO-FIRE switch in flight) for a harder game. Hold the sight on a target to lock rockets on, then fire. The top-right button drops bombs or fires rockets.
Keyboard: arrows or WASD steer, Space fires, F drops ordnance, Shift boosts, P pauses.
